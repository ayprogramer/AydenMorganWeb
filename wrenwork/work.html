Custom automation

Work that runs on its own inside the tools you already use. This one reads incoming mail, catches phishing and payment scams, and quarantines them before anyone clicks. Anything unclear gets held for a person to review.

  • The problem: phishing and fake invoices slip past basic spam filters, and one wrong click is expensive.
  • What it does: checks every message for lookalike domains, spoofed senders, and payment-change requests.
  • What happens next: clear threats are quarantined, borderline ones wait for a person, and IT gets a morning summary.

Full custom software

Software built from scratch around how a business works. This one maps land parcels against nearby gas transmission lines and site constraints, then scores each one so a team knows where to look first. Click a parcel to try it.

  • The problem: teams spend weeks of diligence on sites that were never going to work.
  • What it does: pulls parcel and gas transmission data together and checks each parcel against site rules.
  • What happens next: every parcel gets a score, so the team starts with the best sites.
