Our Solution

Computing in the Cosmos

We're building orbital data centers that leverage the natural vacuum of space—no atmosphere means no heat buildup, unlimited solar energy, and zero impact on Earth's resources.

❄️

Passive Cooling

Space is naturally -270°C. Heat radiates away instantly without any active cooling systems.

☀️

Unlimited Solar

24/7 solar exposure in orbit provides constant, renewable power with no weather interruptions.

🔒

Ultimate Security

Physical isolation from terrestrial threats. The most secure data infrastructure possible.
